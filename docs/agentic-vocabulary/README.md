# Agentic vocabulary

This directory holds the model that the `fal-agentic-vocabulary` and `fal-agentic-audit` skills are based on, and the rules for changing those skills.

- `metamodel.md` defines what the model can contain, including the rules for naming its nodes.
- `model.yaml` holds the model: the biases, the antipatterns, the principles, and their relations.
- `authoring.md` states the rules for the content and the structure of the skills.
- `style.md` states the style conventions of the skill text.

Which documents to read depends on the work:

- A change to the model, or to the content or the structure of either skill: `authoring.md`, `metamodel.md`, and `model.yaml`.
- Any change to the text of either skill, including a check of its wording: `style.md`.
- A reference, in any other skill, to a name that the vocabulary skill defines: the "Names" section of `style.md`.
